## 2. How we'll respond to a NEVER behaviour

*Pick 2 to 4. Tweak the wording. Or write your own.*

☐ **Assume positive intent.** We remind ourselves the team won't be perfect right away, and slip-ups are chances for kind correction and mutual learning.

☐ **Say "oops."** If I catch myself doing a NEVER behaviour, I say *"oops, that's a never behaviour."* Leaders going first here matters most.

☐ **Say "ouch."** If I notice someone else doing one, I say *"ouch, that feels like a NEVER behaviour. I'm mentioning it because I value our work together."*

☐ **Silent signal.** We use an agreed hand sign or emoji (✋ 🚫) to flag it without stopping the flow, in meetings or in chat.

## 3. How we'll respond to an ALWAYS behaviour

*Pick 2 to 4.*

☐ **Name it specifically.** *"That was an ALWAYS behaviour, [name] just [specific action]."* Specific beats generic; "great job" does nothing.

☐ **Signal it lightly.** An agreed emoji (🌟) in chat, or a thumbs-up in person. No interruption needed.

☐ **Pass it forward.** When someone models an ALWAYS behaviour toward me, I do the same for someone else this week.

☐ **Bank it for the check-in.** If the moment passes, note it and raise it in the appreciation round.
