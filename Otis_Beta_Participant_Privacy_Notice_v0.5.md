# Otis beta participant privacy notice (v0.5)

This is the implementation source for the in-app beta notice. It must be reviewed by the organisation's privacy owner before it is used as a final public privacy notice. Version 0.5 replaces version 0.4 for active beta participation because the optional enhanced-audio processor changed from ElevenLabs to Deepgram. A person who acknowledged an earlier beta notice must read and acknowledge this version before continuing.

## What Otis collects

Otis collects assessment responses about your work on the team, the role you choose to provide, and time-zone information where it is available. You may also choose to provide a broad location where you usually work. When Otis recognises a city and country, it uses that selection to determine your IANA time zone automatically; it does not ask for or need a street address. Otis does not ask for age, gender identity, ethnicity, nationality, or other demographic details in the beta interview.

Your name and email are stored separately from your assessment responses. The consultant who invited you can see whether you have participated and completed the assessment, but Otis is designed not to show them a name linked to a particular response.

## How team materials are created

Otis uses team-level patterns, summaries, and paraphrases by default. Exact wording is a separate choice: you can choose summaries only, or allow short exact excerpts without your name. Exact excerpts are never attributed to you by name. In a small team, a colleague may still recognise a distinctive situation or phrase.

Please avoid including other people's private information or unnecessary identifying details in free-text answers.

## AI-assisted analysis and optional enhanced audio

Otis uses external AI services to help analyse de-identified team material. Before external processing, the application removes common direct identifiers, such as known participant names, email addresses, phone numbers, handles, and links where possible. This cannot guarantee that every contextual detail in free text is non-identifying.

Enhanced audio is optional. When it is available and you choose it, Otis uses Deepgram to generate Otis's spoken replies and to turn a recording you choose to make into an editable transcript. Otis holds the recording in memory only long enough to request the transcript and then discards it; Otis does not store raw microphone recordings. Otis stores only the text you choose to submit.

Deepgram is an external processor. It receives your chosen microphone recording for transcription and the text needed to create Otis's spoken reply. It may process request data under its own service terms and privacy practices, including in locations outside your country. You can use text-only input instead, and can change your enhanced-audio choice from your profile at any time.

## Your choices and withdrawal

You must read and acknowledge this notice before the beta interview starts. This one acknowledgement covers your beta journey, including the Results & Team Agreement Activity. Before you share stories or behaviours in that activity, Otis asks you to confirm or change your exact-word setting; this is not a second privacy acknowledgement unless this notice is updated. You can update your exact-word and enhanced-audio choices later. You may request withdrawal of future use through the consultant who invited you. Where a team report has already been generated, material already included in that report may not be removable from that issued report.

## Retention and questions

Beta participant data is reviewed for deletion 12 months after the team's beta participation ends. For questions, corrections, technical support, or a withdrawal request, email [contact@wavelength.team](mailto:contact@wavelength.team?subject=Otis%20privacy%20or%20support%20question). You can also contact the consultant who invited you.

## Before publishing externally

The privacy owner must add and verify the legal entity, privacy contact, external processors and processing locations, the applicable legal basis, the final retention/deletion procedure, and the contractual/data-transfer basis for Deepgram and every other external AI service used by the beta. Do not represent this draft as a complete legal privacy notice until those details are confirmed.
