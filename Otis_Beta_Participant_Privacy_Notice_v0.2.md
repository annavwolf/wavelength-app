# Otis beta participant privacy notice (v0.2 — superseded)

This historical draft was superseded by `Otis_Beta_Participant_Privacy_Notice_v0.3.md` when the beta began collecting an optional city and country to derive a participant's time zone.

This is the implementation source for the in-app beta notice. It must be reviewed by the organisation's privacy owner before it is used as a final public privacy notice.

## What Otis collects

Otis collects assessment responses about your work on the team, the role you choose to provide, and time-zone information. You may also choose to provide a country or broad region. Otis does not ask for age, gender identity, ethnicity, nationality, or other demographic details in the beta interview.

Your name and email are stored separately from your assessment responses. The consultant who invited you can see whether you have participated and completed the assessment, but Otis is designed not to show them a name linked to a particular response.

## How team materials are created

Otis uses team-level patterns, summaries, and paraphrases by default. Exact wording is a separate choice: you can choose summaries only, or allow short exact excerpts without your name. Exact excerpts are never attributed to you by name. In a small team, a colleague may still recognise a distinctive situation or phrase.

Please avoid including other people's private information or unnecessary identifying details in free-text answers.

## AI-assisted analysis and voice input

Otis uses external AI services to help analyse de-identified team material. Before external processing, the application removes common direct identifiers, such as known participant names, email addresses, phone numbers, handles, and links where possible. This cannot guarantee that every contextual detail in free text is non-identifying.

Voice input is optional. If you opt in and tap a microphone control, your browser's speech-recognition provider processes your audio into text. Otis receives the resulting text; it does not record or retain raw microphone audio. You can choose text-only input at any time.

## Your choices and withdrawal

You must read and acknowledge this notice before the beta interview starts. You can update your exact-word and voice-input choices later. You may request withdrawal of future use through the consultant who invited you. Where a team report has already been generated, material already included in that report may not be removable from that issued report.

## Retention and questions

Beta participant data is reviewed for deletion 12 months after the team's beta participation ends. For questions, corrections, technical support, or withdrawal requests, email [contact@wavelength.team](mailto:contact@wavelength.team?subject=Otis%20privacy%20or%20support%20question). You can also contact the consultant who invited you.

## Before publishing externally

The privacy owner must add and verify the legal entity, privacy contact, external processors and processing locations, the applicable legal basis, and the final retention/deletion procedure. Do not represent this draft as a complete legal privacy notice until those details are confirmed.
